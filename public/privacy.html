Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 10, 2026

Resizer Tools is developed and maintained by Saurabh Kumar Sharma. This Privacy Policy outlines our commitment to user privacy, data security, and explains how information is handled when you use our iOS application.

Privacy First: Resizer Tools operates on a 100% on-device processing model. Your documents, photos, signatures, and extracted text never leave your device.

1. Information We Collect

Resizer Tools does not require account creation, login, or registration. We do not collect, store, or sell personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

2. Permissions & Device Access

  • Photos: Used to import images for PDF creation, image-to-PDF conversion, and OCR text recognition.
  • Files & Documents: Used to import, edit, merge, split, compress, and save PDF files locally in your device storage.
  • Camera: Used solely to capture document photos for PDF creation and OCR text recognition.
  • Face ID / Touch ID (Biometrics): Used strictly on-device for the optional App Lock feature to secure your confidential documents. Biometric authentication is handled entirely by Apple's Secure Enclave and is never accessible, collected, or transmitted by the app.

3. 100% On-Device Local Processing

All core functionalities—including PDF merging, splitting, compressing, page reorganizing, watermark stamping, digital signatures, password encryption/decryption, and OCR text recognition—are executed completely locally on your device. No document files are ever uploaded to any external server.

4. Network Connectivity & Daily Quotas

The app checks your network connection status locally to manage the free tier daily usage limit and serve advertisements. Free users receive unlimited tool access with ads when online, and a daily quota of 5 free tool uses when offline. No web browsing activity or external internet traffic is tracked.

5. Advertising & Third-Party Services

The free version of Resizer Tools uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) may collect anonymous device identifiers to serve relevant or non-personalized ads according to your consent choices. Premium PRO subscribers enjoy a 100% ad-free experience.

6. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

All in-app purchases and subscriptions are securely managed by Apple's App Store via StoreKit. We never receive, process, or store your payment card or financial details.

7. Analytics & Diagnostics

To improve app performance and stability, we use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to receive anonymous diagnostic reports and crash logs.
