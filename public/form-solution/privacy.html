Privacy Policy — Form Solution

Effective Date: November 2026

Form Solution is an all-in-one document assistant application for iOS designed to help users prepare photos, signatures, and documents for online applications and forms. Developed by Saurabh Kumar Sharma.

100% On-Device Guarantee: Form Solution processes all photos, signatures, and scanned documents entirely on your local device. Your sensitive files and personal identifiers are never uploaded to any cloud server or database.

1. Information Collection

Form Solution does not require account creation, sign-in, email addresses, or phone numbers. We do not collect, view, or sell any personal documents, certificates, or signatures created within the app.

2. Permissions & Device Access

  • Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription): Used solely to photograph physical documents for scanning, edge correction, and capturing signatures.
  • Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription): Used solely when you manually import photos from your device to crop, resize, or compress them to target KB/MB sizes.

3. Anonymous Diagnostics

We may use anonymous diagnostic tools (such as Firebase Crashlytics) to collect basic crash logs and technical statistics (device model, iOS version) to fix technical bugs. These diagnostics never contain your images, documents, or personal data.
